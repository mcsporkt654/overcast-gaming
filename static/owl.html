The OWL

The OWL is our regularly-scheduled League. Sign up for a chance to test your mettle once a week in pursuit of the fabled OWL Cup. Results will be posted here weekly.

Sign Up on Discord 📋 Latest Updates

League Format

⚔

Weekly Battles

One match per week. Show up, roll dice, and leave it all on the board.

🎯

Round Robin

Face every commander in the league. No safe byes — everyone earns their standing.

🏆

Playoffs

Top commanders advance to playoff brackets. One champion rises. One takes the Cup.

📋

Six Rounds

Six rounds of competitive play. Each round, pairings post to Discord. Results log here.
